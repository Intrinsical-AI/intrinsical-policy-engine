# Synthetic approval record

This fixture describes an invented process item for technical authoring practice.
It records no production assessment and makes no claim of legal correctness.

## Observations

Record the synthetic signoff decision and the limits of that decision.
The example coordinator inspected the submitted item and its documented path.
The mock record identifies the stage being exercised and the next handoff.
The reviewer compared the expected flags, selected work and example dates with
the declared scenario. These observations exist solely to exercise explicit
evidence collection and the resulting file inventory.

## Result and limits

The sample approval record is complete for this synthetic test case. Any open
questions in a real workflow require a named reviewer, actual observations and
an agreed follow-up date. Copying this fixture does not perform that review.
A successful content check or seal does not approve a process or establish
that the statements in a supplied document are accurate.
